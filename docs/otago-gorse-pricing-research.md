# Otago Gorse-Clearance Pricing Research

## Purpose

This working note supports the Gorse Force landing-page price guide. It is not a quote, a formal valuation, or a verified competitor rate card. Published Otago specialist-gorse pricing is limited; the recommended public prices therefore use **New Zealand earthworks benchmarks as a floor**, then apply a premium for specialist vegetation work, gorse density, rural mobilisation, access, safety, and follow-up risk.

## Public reference points

| Source | Relevant evidence | Pricing implication |
|---|---|---|
| Otago Regional Council, *Gorse and broom* | Gorse can grow in most Otago habitat types; it forms dense stands, reduces grazing usability, and has long-lived seeds. | Every quote needs an access, density, terrain, and regrowth allowance rather than a fixed commodity rate. |
| Land Clear, *FAQ* | Mechanical mulching is positioned as a fast method for large, inaccessible gorse; steep or unsafe sites require specialised equipment. | Specialist-machine and difficult-access work should carry a meaningful premium over generic vegetation work. |
| Earthworks Tauranga, *Earthworks Cost NZ* | Published NZ guide: machine plus operator commonly falls around NZ$120–$200/hour, while day rates commonly fall around NZ$800–$1,500. | A specialist Otago gorse-clearance price needs to sit above general plant-hire economics after accounting for additional crew time, fuel, mobilisation, risk, equipment wear, and business overhead. |

## Recommended public positioning

The website should show **starting investments**, not fixed per-hectare or fixed per-hour promises. Each price is displayed as **ex-GST** and subject to a site visit. This protects the quote quality where gorse density, slope, access, disposal, and follow-up scope vary materially.

## Recommended target structure

| Offer | Public starting investment, ex-GST | Use case | Internal target gross margin |
|---|---:|---|---:|
| Gorse Strike | NZ$690 | Small, accessible residential or lifestyle-block patches. | 45–50% |
| Boundary Breaker | NZ$1,290 | Fencelines, access tracks, and boundary recovery. | 45–50% |
| Gorse & Go | NZ$1,890 | Cut, clear, and remove vegetation on a compact job. | 45–50% before abnormal disposal costs |
| Paddock Force | NZ$2,450 / day | Full-day standard machinery-and-crew clearance. | 45–50% |
| Full Force | NZ$3,450 / day | Dense infestation, difficult access, or heavy-clearance setup. | 48–55% |
| Return of the Gorse | NZ$475 | Scheduled follow-up and early-regrowth control. | 50%+ |

The target gross margin is a pricing discipline, not a guarantee of net profit. Before final quotes, Gorse Force should replace these assumptions with its own actual direct labour, plant, fuel, transport, disposal, insurance, and maintenance costs.
