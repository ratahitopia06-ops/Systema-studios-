# Gorse Force Brand Brief

## Primary brand line

> **NO GORSE. NO REMORSE.**

## Supporting lines

- Send in the Gorse Force.
- Of course, we have no remorse for gorse.
- Got gorse? Call the force.
- Gorse gone. Horse on.
- Clear the gorse. Get your land back.

---

## Brand personality

Gorse Force is tough, fast, funny, straight-talking, hard-working, local, and slightly ridiculous—while remaining professional when it matters.

The voice should sound like two hard-working South Island operators who are not afraid of a bloody thorn.

## Brand voice

> “You grow it. We mow it.”
>
> “Thick gorse? No remorse.”
>
> “Any gorse. Any course. Any weather.”
>
> “We smash the gorse so your horse can have a better four-course.”
>
> “Gorse in the paddock? Send in the Force.”

---

## Visual identity

### Logo concept

**GORSE FORCE** should use large, heavy military/action lettering, with the supporting line below it:

> **NO GORSE. NO REMORSE.**

The logo icon should show a stylised gorse bush being attacked by a pair of crossed loppers, with a small lightning bolt or shield behind it.

An alternative icon is a thorny gorse bush inside a circular “force” badge.

### Colour direction

| Colour | Role |
|---|---|
| Black | Provides a rugged contracting and machinery feel. |
| Yellow | References gorse flowers and hazard signage. |
| White | Keeps the identity clean and readable. |

## Uniform

Use black work shirts with a yellow **GORSE FORCE** logo.

| Placement | Copy |
|---|---|
| Back | **NO GORSE. NO REMORSE.** |
| Sleeve | **GORSE FORCE — OTAGO** |

Complete the uniform with work pants, boots, gloves, and proper eye protection. The team should look like a legitimate vegetation-clearance crew rather than gardeners.
